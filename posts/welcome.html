Welcome to My Blog

September 27, 2026

This is the first entry on my new blog. The site is built with pure, semantic HTML and a lightweight stylesheet—no trackers, no heavy JavaScript, and no complex frameworks.

Why Pure HTML?

Writing directly in HTML gives full control over the layout, loads instantly, and keeps publishing simple.

Whenever I want to add a new article, I can duplicate the template, write my thoughts, and push to GitHub.
