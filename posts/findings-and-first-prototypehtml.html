Findings and first prototype

October 30, 2026

After deliberating for some time, I came to the conclusion that I must make a sine-wave emitting egg "doneness" detector.

It works by sending a sine wave through the egg, recording the distortion on a piezoelectric mic, and then using previous tests data to determine whether it is raw, soft-boiled, or hard-boiled.

My first prototype has approximately a 60% success rate which is not exactly what I want.

I tried to use a speaker connected to my computer that would assist in the process but the speaker actually made it less accurate than a simple piezo ring test.

The next step for me is to test out a proper wave generator I bought online and see if that'll make a difference. If it does, I will post the prototype at that time on my next blog.
